## Understanding California Proposition 65 and Heavy Metal Compliance in White Label Lipsticks

California Proposition 65 requires businesses to provide warnings to Californians about significant exposures to chemicals that cause cancer, birth defects, or other reproductive harm. For cosmetics brands selling or distributing lipsticks, lip stains, and glosses into the California market, heavy metal contamination—specifically lead, cadmium, arsenic, and mercury—presents severe legal and financial risks if safe harbor limits are exceeded. Beautychain Limited functions as an uncompromising white label lipstick california prop 65 heavy metal compliant supplier, ensuring that every raw material input is meticulously screened and purified before compounding.

Indie beauty founders expanding their retail footprint into North America must recognize that general cosmetic safety certification is insufficient to protect against Proposition 65 bounty hunter lawsuits. Because lip products are applied directly to the lips and subject to incidental ingestion, regulatory enforcement bodies and private litigants routinely test commercial cosmetic items for trace heavy metals. Beautychain Limited mitigates this liability by implementing rigorous incoming raw material audits, utilizing only high-purity cosmetic-grade mineral colorants, and enforcing strict manufacturing segregation to eliminate cross-contamination during mixing and molding.
