A buyer searching for a lip mud manufacturer for soft matte finish brands is rarely buying colour alone. The real brief concerns how the material spreads under a doe-foot, whether the finish stays visually blurred after 2 hours, and whether the moisturising skin-feel remains acceptable as volatile carriers dissipate. At Beautychain Limited, our approach uses soft-focus microsphere technology to diffuse the appearance of lip lines while balancing emollients so the finish does not usually feel stripped. “Non-drying” should still be treated as a sensory target, not an absolute promise for every user.

Recent 2026 discussion around gloss, stain, tint and oil suggests that shoppers still compare soft matte lip products with fluid formats, especially on comfort and applicator pickup. That affects development. A lip mud that photographs well but drags after 6 swipes is not ready, while a comfortable formula that migrates beyond the lip edge has moved too far in the other direction. Buyers can review our broader [custom lip product formats](/products), but a soft matte system needs its own fill and pack validation.

Colour approval is set at Delta E <= 0.5 under D65 illumination. Between production batches, the working tolerance widens to Delta E <= 1.0 because raw-material variation and thermal history cannot be eliminated completely. Pearlescent systems and high-tint bases are particularly sensitive to fill heat; we have seen a shade approved on a room-temperature bench sample read 2 shades deeper after hot filling. For that reason, approval should be made against a filled, cooled unit rather than material spread directly from a laboratory beaker.

Dispersion quality is just as important. An under-milled anhydrous colour base may look uniform on day 1, then settle over 10–14 days and create a visible gradient inside the tube. We hold development samples long enough to catch that movement before confirming the master standard. The brand receives a physical control sample, and Beautychain retains the matched reference for production comparison under D65.

Our normal sample timing is 7 working days from an approved brief. If the buyer selects an existing colour card and does not request a structural formula change, the first sample can usually leave in 3 working days. The sample fee is refunded against the commercial order, and the [sample request desk](/samples) should receive the target shade reference, market destination and preferred pack before laboratory work begins. The pilot MOQ is 500 pieces per shade, but this is a filling MOQ rather than a custom packaging MOQ. Most component suppliers begin at 3,000–5,000 units, so a 500-piece trial normally uses an available stock tube and standard applicator. This distinction matters. We have seen buyers approve a 500-unit launch plan and then lose 5 weeks because the selected cap required a separate production run.

For wand-based gloss used as a fluid benchmark, the practical range is usually 1,200–2,500 mPa·s at 25 °C. Below 1,200 mPa·s, the material tends to run off the applicator; above 2,500 mPa·s, it may stop flowing back into the tube and leave an empty-looking channel. A doe-foot generally provides about 300 mPa·s more working headroom than a brush because it carries material in its flocked surface. Lip mud is assessed by spread, pickup and recovery rather than by copying a gloss number without context.

Flavour oils also need pack contact testing. Vanilla and peppermint oils can plasticise some TPE applicators over 8–10 weeks, producing a sticky tip during a stability pull even though the initial bench sample looked normal. Changing the flavour percentage may solve it, but in some cases the correct fix is a different applicator compound. The [customization route](/customization) should therefore lock formula and applicator together.

Beautychain’s stock packaging library includes 5 ml and 6 ml round PETG tubes with screw caps, a 4.5 ml slim tube and an 8 ml squeeze tube with a slant tip. A 3.5 ml ABS stick is available for firmer soft matte formats. PCR PETG can be specified at 30% or 50%, although clarity drops visibly at 50%, which is relevant if the brand expects an exact visual match between the online render and the filled pack.

A refillable aluminium shell uses a PP inner cup, meaning the aluminium does not touch the formula. Bamboo-clad ABS caps are also possible, but they carry a separate supplier lead time of 4–5 weeks. In our experience, that cap is the single most common source of a slipped launch date. The shell may be ready while the decorative cap is still at the component supplier. Fill control matters even where the consumer never sees the number. On a 6 ml gloss, our normal fill-weight tolerance is +/-3%, and the declared net content must remain valid at the lower bound rather than only at the nominal setting. A lip mud pack requires the same net-content discipline, with the target adjusted for formula density. Beautychain checks start-up units and scheduled line samples under the documented [production process](/process).

Stability work normally includes 12 weeks of accelerated storage at 45 °C and 3 freeze-thaw cycles between -10 °C and 25 °C, with each stage held for 24 hours. Thermal shock adds 24 hours at 50 °C followed by 24 hours at -5 °C for 3 cycles. The evaluation focuses on leakage and label lift, while formula observations record separation, odour change, shade drift and applicator condition.

Every commercial batch is checked for Pb, Cd, Hg and As, alongside microbial controls. Those controls cover TPC and yeast-and-mould counts, while S. aureus and P. aeruginosa must be absent. ISO 11930 challenge testing applies only to formulas containing water, which is one practical reason many new brands launch an anhydrous lip mud first. The decision does not remove ordinary hygiene controls or batch testing.

ISO 22716 applies to the manufacturing plant, not to an individual lip mud. For the European Union, CPSR Part A contains the formula safety assessment, while Part B contains the plant GMP statement. Part A by itself will not place the product legally on the EU market. The EU Responsible Person files the CPNP notification before sale; the portal filing is free, while the CPSR commonly costs about EUR 400–900 per formula.

US MoCRA requires facility registration and product listing. It also requires a responsible person to be identified on the label. MoCRA does not create an “export compliant” status for an ordinary cosmetic, and Beautychain does not describe custom lip products that way. MSDS and COA files are provided on qualified commercial inquiry, with applicable test reports supplied for the confirmed formula and batch.

COSMOS and ECOCERT concern the formula and raw-material chain, including a minimum of 95% ingredients of natural origin plus processing restrictions. They do not certify a factory merely because it holds ISO 22716. A plant ISO certificate should never be presented as an eco certificate. Under EU Regulation (EC) No 1223/2009, “organic” is not itself a regulated cosmetic claim, so legal label review and a COSMOS audit remain 2 separate tests.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Q: What is the MOQ for a custom soft matte lip mud?

The pilot MOQ is 500 pieces per shade, using an available stock pack in most cases. That quantity applies to filling, not to newly manufactured components, which usually begin at 3,000–5,000 units. A 500-piece pilot is useful for buyer review, content production and controlled market testing, but it should not be interpreted as a promise of 500 custom-moulded tubes.

### Q: How quickly can Beautychain produce a colour sample?

Beautychain normally needs 7 working days after the brief is approved, or 3 working days if the buyer selects an existing colour card without changing the base. The sample fee is refunded against the order. Approval is measured at Delta E <= 0.5 under D65, and we recommend checking the filled unit after it has cooled rather than approving a wet drawdown alone.

### Q: Which EU and US compliance documents are needed?

For the EU, the core route includes CPSR Part A, CPSR Part B and notification by the EU Responsible Person through CPNP before market placement. For the US, MoCRA requires facility registration and product listing, with the responsible person identified on the label. ISO 22716 supports the factory GMP position but does not replace either market process.

### Q: Can a soft matte lip mud provide comfortable moisturising skin-feel?

An anhydrous formula can be adjusted for cosmetic conditioning, barrier hydration and a less stripped after-feel by balancing the soft-focus microsphere system with compatible emollients. Beautychain does not make universal comfort claims because climate, application thickness and individual lip condition vary. The 12-week stability programme also checks whether that sensory balance changes at 45 °C.

### Q: Which package is safest for a 500-piece pilot?

A stock 4.5 ml, 5 ml or 6 ml PETG tube is usually the lowest-risk choice for a 500-piece pilot because the pack is already available for compatibility work. The selected doe-foot still needs testing against the formula, especially where vanilla or peppermint flavour oil is used. PCR PETG at 30% is possible for some projects, while the visible clarity loss at 50% should be approved with a physical sample.
