## Formulation Science: Viscosity, Stability, and Pigment Dispersion

Formulating high-performance lip products demands precise control over lipid phase ratios, wax crystallization temperatures, and pigment wetting agents. Beautychain Limited formulates lipsticks using balanced combinations of candelilla wax, carnauba wax, microcrystalline structures, and emollient esters like jojoba oil or squalane to ensure smooth glide and comfortable wear. Pigment dispersion is optimized through high-shear triple-roll milling, which eliminates agglomerates and achieves uniform color payoff across every single bullet.

Thermal stability is a primary metric evaluated during raw material stress testing. Beautychain Limited subjects pilot batches to continuous 45°C thermal cycling over 14 days to monitor for syneresis (oil sweating), blooming, or structural deformation.
