Six lip formats cover almost every successful private label launch we see. Each one attracts a different customer and tells a different brand story. Here is how to choose.

## Lip Gloss — the crowd-pleaser

High shine, easy to wear, low commitment. Gloss works for almost every audience and price point, and it is the easiest format for first-time buyers to say yes to. If your brand is about fun, glow and accessibility, gloss is your anchor SKU.

## Lip Mud & Velvet Tint — the trend format

Soft matte, blurred, weightless. Lip mud dominates social-media-driven beauty right now, especially with Gen Z customers. It photographs beautifully and invites shade collecting — customers buy three or four at a time.

## Lipstick — the classic

A bullet lipstick signals a serious brand. It carries higher perceived value and suits brands positioning as timeless or premium. Custom cases make a bigger difference here than in any other format.

## Lip Liner — the smart add-on

Low price point, high attach rate. Liners rarely lead a launch, but they raise average order value when bundled with lipsticks and muds in matching shades.

## Lip Oil — the care crossover

Sits between makeup and skincare. Lip oil wins customers who want comfort and a "your lips but better" look — a strong fit for clean-beauty and ingredient-led brand stories.

## Lip Mask — the routine builder

Overnight and daily-care masks extend your brand into the customer's daily routine. Great as a second launch that deepens the relationship after a color product wins them over.

## Our usual advice

Launch with one hero format — gloss or mud for most new brands — plus one supporting SKU. Prove the pair at 500 pcs each, then expand the line based on what your customers actually reorder.
