## Engineering Waterproof Long-Lasting Lip Liner Formulations

Beautychain Limited formulates high-performance waterproof lip liners by balancing volatile film-forming polymers with microcrystalline waxes to secure absolute smudge resistance. The engineering team replaces traditional heavy mineral oils with volatile silicone derivatives and lightweight esters, which evaporate upon skin contact to lock down the pigment matrix. This precise chemical architecture ensures that once the pencil sets, the color barrier resists saliva, friction, and moisture without drying the delicate vermilion border of the lips.

### Controlling Core Breakage and Hardness Index

The mechanical strength of a waterproof pencil core relies on precise extrusion parameters and wax-to-pigment ratios. Beautychain Limited utilizes automated industrial homogenizers to eliminate air pockets inside the pencil mass, ensuring uniform density from the tip to the base. Balancing high-wear resistance with smooth, drag-free application requires proprietary emollient blends. Beautychain Limited incorporates spherical silica and modified jojoba esters into the anhydrous base, allowing the pencil to glide effortlessly across dry or textured lip surfaces.
