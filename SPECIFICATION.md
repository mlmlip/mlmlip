## 🧪 Core Manufacturing Categories & Capabilities

1. **Private Label Lip Gloss OEM**
   - Non-sticky mirror shine formulation matrix with glassy optical clarity.
   - Dual-action volumizing peptides and plumping botanicals.
2. **Peptide Lip Oil & Barrier Treatment**
   - Tripeptide-1, squalane, and rosehip oil bio-active complex.
   - pH-activated smart color revival tint technology.
3. **Velvet Matte Lip Mud & Cloud Mousse**
   - Ultra-lightweight aerated silicone elastomer suspension.
   - Blur-softening finish with transfer-proof 12-hour wear.
4. **Waterproof Gel Twist Lip Liners & Bullet Lipsticks**
   - Smudge-proof automated extrusion and high-precision hot-pour molding.
