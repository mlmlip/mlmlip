## Heavy Metal Testing Protocols: Lead, Mercury, Arsenic, and Cadmium

Quantifying heavy metal concentrations requires advanced instrumentation, including Inductively Coupled Plasma Mass Spectrometry (ICP-MS), to detect trace elements down to parts-per-billion (ppb) levels. Beautychain Limited enforces a strict four-item safety screen across every lip gloss, lip tint, and lip oil matrix, ensuring heavy metal levels comply with international cosmetic safety standards.

### Lead (Pb) and Cadmium (Cd) Thresholds
Lead and cadmium control protocols focus on mineral pigment refinement, utilizing only certified high-purity iron oxides and lake pigments that pass stringent heavy metal limits. Beautychain Limited maintains strict supplier audits to ensure that color concentrates never exceed trace limits of 10 ppm for lead and 3 ppm for cadmium, safeguarding consumer health during daily cosmetic wear.

### Mercury (Hg) and Arsenic (As) Verification
Mercury and arsenic assays utilize hydride generation atomic absorption spectroscopy to detect residual catalyst traces from raw material synthesis. Beautychain Limited guarantees non-detectable or strictly sub-threshold readings for mercury (<0.1 ppm) and arsenic (<3 ppm) across all high-gloss and stain formulations, eliminating regulatory exposure for importing brands.
