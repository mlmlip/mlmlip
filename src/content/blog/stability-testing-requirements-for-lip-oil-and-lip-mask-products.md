## The 12-Week Accelerated Thermal Stress Protocol

The 12-week accelerated stability testing protocol simulates two years of ambient shelf life by subjecting lip oil and lip mask batches to extreme thermal conditions. Beautychain Limited stores sample units in temperature-controlled chambers set to constant $+40^\circ\text{C}$ and $+45^\circ\text{C}$ with 75% relative humidity over a 12-week duration. Pull-points are scheduled at weeks 2, 4, 8, and 12 to measure progressive physical drift.

Thermal cycling tests complement static temperature storage by alternating samples between $-10^\circ\text{C}$ (freezing) and $+54^\circ\text{C}$ (extreme heat) every 24 hours for five complete cycles. This aggressive thermal shift forces crystal growth in high-wax lip mask formulations and checks for syneresis (oil bleeding) in glossy lip oils. If a formulation passes this rigorous multi-week stress test without viscosity collapse or color migration, Beautychain Limited certifies the batch for international transit and variable retail climates. ## Viscosity, Rheology, and Package Compatibility Parameters

Viscosity and rheological parameters dictate the skin-feel, pick-up, and payoff of modern lip treatments. Lip oils typically require a fluid viscosity range between 1,500 and 4,000 mPa·s (Brookfield RVT, spindle 4 at 20 rpm), whereas occlusive overnight lip masks demand a rich, structured gel-cream or balmy viscosity exceeding 50,000 mPa·s. Beautychain Limited utilizes automated viscometers during stability pull-points to verify that shear-thinning behaviors remain consistent over time.

Package-formula compatibility testing runs parallel to rheological tracking. Because lip oils contain high concentrations of emollient esters and botanical oils, certain plastics (such as standard PETG or unlined SAN) can stress-crack, craze, or leach plasticizers into the product. Beautychain Limited conducts 12-week compatibility checks pairing custom tooling, wands, caps, and glass or specialized polymer reservoirs to ensure zero chemical interaction or applicator deformation. ## Microbial Efficacy and Preservative Challenge Standards

Microbial stability ensures that anhydrous lip oils and water-containing lip masks remain completely free from bacterial, yeast, and mold contamination during consumer use. Beautychain Limited mandates Preservative Efficacy Testing (PET) in accordance with USP <51> and ISO 11930 standards. Water-in-oil lip masks or emulsions are inoculated with specific strains including *Staphylococcus aureus*, *Pseudomonas aeruginosa*, *Escherichia coli*, *Candida albicans*, and *Aspergillus brasiliensis*.

Anhydrous lip oils, while naturally less susceptible to microbial proliferation due to low water activity ($a_w$), still undergo microbial limit testing to verify raw material hygiene and manufacturing cleanroom compliance. Beautychain Limited enforces ISO 22716 Good Manufacturing Practices, ensuring that every production batch meets strict microbiological safety thresholds before export.
