Most cosmetic factories quote minimums of 5,000 to 20,000 pieces per SKU. For a new brand, that is not a minimum — it is a bet. You are committing thousands of dollars before a single customer has touched your product.

A 500 pcs minimum changes that equation completely. Here is how to think about your first run.

## Why small first runs win

**You test the market, not your luck.** With 500 pieces of a lip gloss, you can sell through your first batch in weeks, collect real customer feedback, and reorder the winners — instead of sitting on 15,000 units of a shade nobody wanted.

**You can launch more than one SKU.** The budget that buys one 10,000 pcs run can instead launch three or four shades at 500 pcs each. Shade variety is one of the strongest conversion drivers in lip products.

**Cash stays liquid.** Inventory is frozen cash. Smaller runs mean faster cycles: sell, learn, reorder, scale.

## What to prepare before you talk to a manufacturer

1. **Your format** — lip gloss, lip mud, lip liner, lipstick, lip oil or lip mask. Not sure? A good manufacturer will recommend a format based on your market and price point.
2. **Your price point** — this determines packaging and formulation choices more than anything else.
3. **Reference products** — photos or links of products whose texture, finish or packaging you like.
4. **Your brand assets** — logo and brand colors, so sampling can include real branding from day one.

## What the process looks like

With a manufacturer that handles packaging, formulation and branding under one roof, the path is: brief → quotation → samples in about 7 days → revisions → production → QC → shipping. At MLM Lip, the sampling fee is deducted from your order, so sampling effectively costs nothing.

The goal of your first 500 pcs is not profit — it is proof. Prove the product sells, then scale with confidence.
